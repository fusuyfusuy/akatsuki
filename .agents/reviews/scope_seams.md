## E. Persistence / transaction seams
22. **Readers write the projection without the vault lock; scan is outside the tx.** All 10 `open_synced_db` callers ([index:126-130](file:///home/devhax/projects/fusuyfusuy/akatsuki/src/index/mod.rs#L126-L130)) run `sync_vault_index`; hashes and file scan are taken at [index:286-398](file:///home/devhax/projects/fusuyfusuy/akatsuki/src/index/mod.rs#L286-L398), the (deferred) tx opens at [index:442](file:///home/devhax/projects/fusuyfusuy/akatsuki/src/index/mod.rs#L442). 23. **Schema rebuild is not atomic** vs ADR-0004 "can never leave a half-migrated index" ([decisions.md:25](file:///home/devhax/projects/fusuyfusuy/akatsuki/.agents/decisions.md#L25)): DROPs, version INSERT, CREATEs are three autocommit batches ([index:146-260](file:///home/devhax/projects/fusuyfusuy/akatsuki/src/index/mod.rs#L146-L260)).
